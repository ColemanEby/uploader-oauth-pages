Privacy Policy

ReplayGremlin Uploader. Last updated 2026-10-18.

Who uses it

This app has one user: the owner of the YouTube channel @ReplayGremlin. It is not available to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's consent, through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, the app:

  • uploads videos and sets thumbnails on the owner's channel;
  • edits the owner's own video details and posts comments as the channel;
  • reads the channel's own videos, comments and analytics (views, watch time, audience).

How it is stored and shared

The sign-in token and the channel's statistics are stored only on the owner's private computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from any other person's Google account is collected.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Third-party access. Revoking deletes the app's ability to use the token, and the owner deletes the stored copy.

YouTube and Google terms

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
